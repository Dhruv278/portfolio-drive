# How I work

In my first thirty days on a new product I read the code and the open tickets before proposing anything, write down the three biggest risks and agree them with the team, ship one visible fix in week one, put an evaluation or a test around the riskiest path, and set a weekly demo of working software with a written note of what changed and why.

I use Claude Code, Codex and Cursor every day, and I write the prompts, evaluations and tooling the team builds on.

I work remotely from Surat, India, with full overlap with Europe and three to four hours of overlap with US East. I am open to contract, employer of record or relocation.
