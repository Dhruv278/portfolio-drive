# Third-party assets

## Models

All 3D models and palette textures under `public/models/` come from Kenney (https://kenney.nl) and are released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain). No attribution is required. Attribution is given here anyway because the work is excellent.

Kits used:

- City Kit (Suburban) 2.0
- City Kit (Commercial) 2.1
- City Kit (Industrial) 2.0
- Nature Kit
- Car Kit 3.0

Only the models the scene references are shipped. The full kits can be re-downloaded from kenney.nl. The original zips are kept locally under `assets/src/` and are not committed.
