# How Dhruv works

Written specification before code, with acceptance criteria agreed up front. Tenant scoping, rate limits and audit trails by default in anything multi-tenant. Evaluations and tests around the paths that can hurt a customer: AI output, payments, permissions. Claude Code, Codex and Cursor every day, and he writes the prompts and tooling the team builds on. A QA round is not done until the tracker says so.

In the first thirty days on a new product he reads the code and the open tickets before proposing anything, writes down the three biggest risks and agrees them with the team, ships one visible fix in week one, puts an evaluation or a test around the riskiest path, and sets a weekly demo of working software with a written note of what changed and why.

He works remotely from Surat, India, with full overlap with Europe and three to four hours of overlap with US East. He is open to contract, employer of record or relocation.
