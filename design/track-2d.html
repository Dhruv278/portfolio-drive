Checkpoint 3 of 6. Own product

Agents that stop when a step fails.

The problem. One failed step halfway through a long agent run used to waste every step before it, and users did not want to hand over their API keys.

The call. Run the agents as a dependency graph, save every step before the next starts, halt on failure. Bring-your-own-key, encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with limits enforced in the service layer.

What shipped. AgentFlow on NestJS and Next.js 14, runs streamed over SSE from Redis pub/sub, three plans on Stripe.
